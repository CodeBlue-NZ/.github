# CodeBlue

Welcome to the **CodeBlue** GitHub organization — the public home for FUJIFILM CodeBlue Limited engineering collaboration in New Zealand.

We design, build, and operate technology services spanning cybersecurity, cloud & infrastructure, data protection, and modern workplace platforms.

---

## What we do

* **Cybersecurity** — Managed security services, detection tooling, and protective controls.
* **Cloud & infrastructure** — Cloud platforms, infrastructure as code, and NZ-aware hosting patterns.
* **Data backup & recovery** — Backup and disaster recovery automation for resilient operations.
* **Modern workplace** — Microsoft 365 and business productivity integrations that keep teams moving.

Learn more about CodeBlue at [codeblue.co.nz](https://www.codeblue.co.nz).

---

## Working with us

If you are an external vendor, contractor, or customer partner with access to repositories in this organization:

* Access is limited to the repositories assigned to your engagement.
* Do not commit credentials, secrets, environment files, or customer personal information.
* Keep technical discussion in **GitHub Issues** or **Discussions** on the assigned repository.
* Collaboration remains subject to applicable MSAs and NDAs.

---

## Security

If you believe you have found a security issue related to CodeBlue systems or code, please email **security@codeblue.co.nz**. Do not open a public issue for suspected vulnerabilities.
